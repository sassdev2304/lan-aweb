## Brand (extracted from logo)
- Canvas: near-black navy `#010715`. Logo blue: `#1353B4` rising into bright electric blue.
- Mark: "LW" where the last stroke of the W shoots upward like a launch. The whole site borrows that stroke as its trajectory line.
- Hard rules: no invented numbers or stats. CTA is WhatsApp `31 9678-2358`, no prefilled message.

## Brief
- **What/for whom:** Lança Web builds landing pages for small businesses (local shops, clinics) that are not online yet.
- **Belief at the end:** "Sem site, meu cliente está indo para o concorrente, e a Lança Web resolve isso rápido."
- **One action, one label everywhere:** **Lançar meu site** → WhatsApp.

## Feeling curve (emotion + on-screen cause)
| Act | Emotion | On-screen cause |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Ignição (hero) | Unease (sub-intense) | A search bar types "loja perto de mim"; the results come back and the visitor's business is not there. |
| 2 Atraso | Sting / feeling late | Pinned lines assemble word by word: he searched, did not find you, and called the competitor. |
| 3 Virada | Quiet hope (authored silence) | Empty space and one input: "Qual o nome da sua empresa?" Nothing moves. |
| 4 Lançamento (**PEAK**) | Ownership / awe | The visitor's own site builds itself with their name, from wireframe to finished, then launches upward along the LW stroke. |
| 5 Prova | Trust | Two real, live client sites run inside device frames on a horizontal rail. |
| 6 Pouso (close) | Calm resolve | Stillness in orbit, one sentence, one button. |

**Peak sentence:** "The moment the visitor's own business name appears on a finished site and it launches." Act 4 has the largest span (3.6vh) and is preceded by the quietest act (3).

**Experience sentence:** It's the site where **you scroll and watch your own company's website get built and launched**.

**Authored silences:** Act 3 (no motion, just the question). The first beat of Act 6 (stars drift, nothing else).

**Leaving without contact:** when the tab loses focus, the tab title becomes "Seu concorrente já tem site." This covers the brief's "se ele sair... perdendo clientes".
